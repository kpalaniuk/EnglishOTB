# Surface: home (index.html) — and the site shell every page inherits

Mode: Persuade (home, ESS, pricing, Inglês, podcast). Read (blog, legacy pages, terms).

Audience: adult non-native English speakers, phone-first, arriving from Instagram/WhatsApp/podcast. Job: believe Jennifer's *system* (not lessons) is the thing that will unstick them, then message her on WhatsApp or enroll. Proof: her own story (learner, lived abroad), the 3 components, student quotes with name + country, podcast reach (131 countries / 1.16M downloads), the Learn-with-Me videos.

Constraints: yellow + white, sunshine, no gradients, no AI-slop imagery, keep her photos, her copy verbatim (ads removed), static HTML, all external links preserved.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A sunroom of framed mid-century posters — English learning as something warm, bright and hand-made, not a school. Refuses the coaching-site default (hero photo + three icon cards + testimonial slider on white) and its opposite (dark, "premium" mentor site).

OWN-WORLD: Sunflower yellow (#F5C542) fields that own whole sections on warm white (#FFFDF7); sage (#A9B89B) and clay (#D4784A) as the flat-illustration palette; charcoal ink (#2B2A28). Thin-frame poster panels (2px ink frame, white mat) hold every photo and illustration. Hand-lettered display voice (Shantell Sans, matching the refreshed logo) over a rounded humanist body (Nunito). Pill buttons with a solid ink shadow-offset-free depth via a 1px ink rule. Sun-ray SVG corners as the only ornament. Flat paper-cut illustrations (Higgsfield, Nano Banana Pro) of the open box, globe, cactus, mic, speech bubbles.

STORY: "You have big dreams in English → you're still stuck → yes, it's possible → here's Jennifer, here's the 3-part system → students say it works → watch the series or message me on WhatsApp."
