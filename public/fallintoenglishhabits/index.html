Fall into English Habits — 21 Day Speaking Challenge

A 21-day challenge

This page is from an earlier chapter of English Outside the Box and is kept here for reference. For current programs, see The English Success System.

Most English learners do not use English enough throughout the week
to improve their conversational skills and actually speak with the confidence
and ease they so deeply desire.

Do you?

In order to speak better English, you need to be speaking every day.

In order to build confidence and reduce fear, you need to face + overcome these obstacles.

In order to use English as naturally and freely as you want to, you need to create the habits of thinking, speaking, and using English every day.

Welcome to the Fall into English Habits: 21 Day Speaking Challenge

In this 21-day challenge, you will:

  • get daily {English} speaking prompts to answer in our group WhatsApp, so that you can improve your speaking, listening, vocabulary, and pronunciation skills
  • practice speaking in daily conversational classes with me, a native-speaking English teacher
  • increase your confidence by discovering your strengths and getting a lot of practice
  • decrease your fear by facing your obstacles and crushing them
  • build the necessary habits to start thinking in English every day and using English more often in your life
  • have fun

You will improve your speaking skills. You will improve your confidence. You will build lasting habits to continue improving your English even after the challenge ends.

You need this English program if you are not using English every day and you say "I want to speak like a native speaker." **

This program is for you if:

  • you are an English learner with at least an intermediate level
  • you need better English skills for more opportunities at work, in university, while traveling, or daily life
  • you have a busy schedule and need the flexibility of speaking on the go and different class time options
  • you ever say "I want to speak like a native speaker"**

**NOTE: this challenge is not designed to get you to speak like a native speaker, but to build the skills necessary to continue improving to that high level of proficiency.

All of this training is done 100% online and can be completed in your home, office, in a local library, on vacation, or from anywhere you are in the world! All you need is a laptop or tablet, an internet connection, and the motivation to learn and complete the daily speaking tasks + as many of the daily classes as you'd like or can!

By learning 'Outside the Box' with me, you are going to get out of the traditional classroom and learn in a new way.

  • Forget the irrelevant textbooks
  • Forget the boring material
  • Forget a restricted schedule
  • Forget driving to an English school.

Throughout this challenge, you will be connecting with me on WhatsApp and in our daily speaking classes.
