FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Jennifer, why can't I ask you for free?

I have over 30,000 people subscribed/following my Instagram, Facebook, and email list. I receive hundreds of questions every week and I physically can not answer every direct message and email that comes to me.

So, I want to help students serious about learning and students who value and respect me, my time and my expertise in English teaching and education.

When can I ask questions?

You can send your questions at any time of the week and I'll review and answer them on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays!

That way, you can get help throughout the week and sometimes, instantly!

What kind of questions can I ask?

You can ask general English questions, including those related to grammar, vocabulary, idioms, slang, etc...

*Note: some questions may be too complex to be answered in a message. In this case, I'll give suggestions for materials and further education.
