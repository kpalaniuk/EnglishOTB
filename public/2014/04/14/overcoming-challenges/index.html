10 Famous People Who Overcame Substantial Challenges

Obstacles and challenges are a part of life. When we are going through life trying to achieve our goals, it is inevitable that something will get in our way, sometime, on our journey to success and happiness. So when we come face to face with these obstacles (things that get in our way, or are difficult to overcome) what should we do? Give up and walk away? Of course not! These obstacles are necessary in life to help us see what is important, help us know what is worth fighting for, and what is worth hard work and determination. Chances are, if something is not important, you won’t fight for it.

So, what has been your obstacle? What has gotten in your way of achieving your dream or reaching success? Share your story here. What was the obstacle and how did you overcome it? Sharing your story could inspire or help another reader reach their “lemonade.” ((this comes from the expression: When life hands you lemons, make lemonade.”))

Are you looking to improve reading comprehension? Check out this article:  10 Famous People Who Overcame Substantial Challenges  via “MastersChannel” After reading about these 10 famous people, choose 3 and summarize their story. Show your understanding of what you read, and practice your writing by using your own words to describe 3 of these famous people, their challenges, and how they overcame them. Feel free to do more research on-line, and include that information, too!

Happy reading, writing, and studying! ♥
