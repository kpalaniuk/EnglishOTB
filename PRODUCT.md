## Product

English Outside the Box — Jennifer Nascimento's online English coaching practice (since 2014, San Diego / Tucson-based, teaches internationally). Flagship offer is **The English Success System**: a Positive-Psychology + English coaching program sold as self-study ($97/mo), group (waitlist), and 1:1 ($600–650/mo). Secondary surfaces: podcast (English Outside the Box Podcast, formerly English Across the Pond — 131 countries, 1.16M downloads), a Portuguese-speaker pronunciation course, a 2014–2023 blog (270 posts), a YouTube channel, and a personal "Night Connections" eBook whose proceeds help pay for her prosthetic leg and cancer scans.

## Users

Primary: adult non-native English speakers (many Brazilian/Portuguese-speaking, also Russian, Italian, etc.), often already living or working in the US, intermediate+ level, who feel stuck/anxious speaking despite years of study. They arrive from Instagram/YouTube/podcast/WhatsApp, on phones, and want to know: who is Jennifer, does her method work, how do I start.

Job: decide to message Jennifer on WhatsApp or enroll in a program.

## Mechanism / position

Not "English lessons" — a *system* that fixes mindset (Confidence Formula), aligns goals with study strategy (Goals that Come Alive), and multiplies daily English use 4× (Everyday English Method). Confidence is more than skills; dreams are more than English. Jennifer herself is the proof: a language learner (Portuguese/Spanish), lived in Spain/Australia/Brazil, married to a Brazilian, TESOL-certified since 2007.

## Voice
